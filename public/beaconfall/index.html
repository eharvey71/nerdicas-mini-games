BEACONFALL

HELP
  • The lighthouse is dark and a ship is coming. Get the lamp lit before it arrives.
  • Everything you can do is marked in the picture and has a button under it. Double-lined buttons walk somewhere, blue ones look, gold ones use or open.
  • Everything costs minutes. Walking costs most. Wrong guesses on a lock or lever cost extra.
  • Turning wheels, dials and switches is free. Only pulling the lever or handle takes time.
  • What you read is copied into the NOTEBOOK, so you do not have to remember it.
  • The lamp needs three things: a lens, power from the engine, and the right colour of glass.
ALL THE RULES

THE GOAL

  • Light the lamp at the top of the lighthouse before the ship is due.
  • CALM has no clock. DUSK gives you time to spare. GALE gives you barely enough.
  • The clock is set from how long this particular island needs, so every game is fair.

THE ISLAND

  • Nine places: the jetty, a sea cave, the crossways, the boathouse, the weather station, the tide house, the lighthouse door, the engine room and the lamp room.
  • The places are always the same. What changes each game: the codes, the symbols, where the key is hidden, and which container holds which part.

WHAT THINGS COST

  • Walking between places: 4 minutes. The cave path and the stairs: 3. Stepping through the lighthouse door: 2.
  • Reading something: 1 minute. Searching a hiding place: 3. Opening a container: 1.
  • Tide lever: 2 minutes if the wheels are right, 5 if not.
  • Door handle: 1 minute if the dials are right, 3 if not.
  • Starting the engine: 2 minutes if the switches are right, 4 if not.
  • Lamp lever: 1 minute, or 5 if the glass is the wrong colour.

THE PUZZLES

  • A brass key is hidden in one of four places. The locked cabinet has a hint scratched on it.
  • The tide house wheels must show the three LOW WATER marks. Somewhere there is a tide chart.
  • The lighthouse door has three dials. The plate on the door names three instruments, in order. The readings are at the weather station.
  • The engine needs a crank, fuel, and four switches set the right way. The switch pattern is chalked inside one of the containers.
  • The lamp needs a lens and the harbour's colour of glass. The harbour flies a pennant.

OTHER THINGS

  • The game saves after every action. Close the page and it will be where you left it.
  • Tap the log to make it taller, and again to shrink it.
  • GIVE UP ends the game. It asks you to tap twice.
  • Your best time to spare is kept for DUSK and for GALE.
