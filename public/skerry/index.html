SKERRY

HELP
  • The beacon is dark. Take a shard from each of three lairs, then light the beacon.
  • Walk into a monster to fight it. Stand on a town or lair and press ENTER.
  • A lair's shard is held by its guardian, shown in magenta. The chest is only a bonus.
  • You are cyan. Monsters are red, towns pink, lairs purple, the beacon pale blue, mountains grey, the sea deep blue.
  • Towns heal you and sell food, potions, weapons and armor.
  • QUEST tells you what is left and which way to go.
ALL THE RULES

GOAL

The beacon that keeps the fog off the island has gone dark. Three shards of its flame lie in three lairs: a cave, a ruin and a barrow. Kill each lair's guardian to take its shard. With all three, enter the beacon, kill the Fog King, and the game is won.

MOVING

  • The arrow buttons, the arrow keys or WASD move you one square.
  • Sea and mountains cannot be crossed.
  • The middle button enters the place you stand on, leaves a lair from its doorway, or waits a turn.
  • MAP shows the parts of the island you have seen.

FIGHTING

Monsters move when you do. Walk into one to strike it; it strikes back when it is next to you. Better weapons hit harder, and armor takes points off every blow you receive. A potion heals you and uses your turn.

FOOD AND DAYS

Walking the island eats rations, faster in forest, hills and swamp. With no food you lose health as you walk. Every 200 steps is a day, and each day the fog makes the island's monsters stronger, so do not dawdle.

TOWNS

Rest to heal, buy rations and potions, and trade up your weapon and armor one grade at a time. RUMOUR is free and marks the next place you need on your map.

LAIRS

The cave is the easiest, then the ruin, then the barrow. Each has lesser monsters (red), a chest, and its guardian (magenta) at the far end. Only killing the guardian gives the shard; the status panel shows the guardian's health while you are inside.

You can walk back out through the doorway at any time. If you do, the guardian and the lesser monsters are back at full strength next time, but an opened chest stays empty.

LEVELS

Kills give experience. Each level adds health and a little damage.

SAVING

The game saves itself as you walk the island, on this device. A lair is not saved part-way. Death ends the run and a new island is made.
