F.U.N. RUNNER

HELP
  • Your sled goes by itself. You only steer, left and right, round the inside of the tunnel.
  • Reach the white ring at the end before TIME runs out. Four tunnels win the league.
  • Gold pads boost you hard for two seconds, and while boosting you smash through blocks and purple sleds. Gates still stop you.
  • On bends, ride the blue line high on the outside wall for extra speed.
  • Pink blocks and gates and purple sleds cost you speed. Gates have one gap, marked in gold.
  • Five cyan stars charge a SHOCK RING. It clears everything pink, purple or orange just ahead.
  • Open-air stretches have no roof. Stay below the white rim or you lose speed.
  • The white tick on the bar at the top is the clock. Stay ahead of it.
ALL THE RULES

THE GOAL

  • A league is four heats. Each heat is a new tunnel, built from sections in a random order.
  • Finish a heat before the clock reaches zero. Run out of time and the league is over.
  • Each later heat is longer and a little faster, with more in the way.
  • ROOKIE, PRO and ELITE leagues are faster in turn and leave less time to spare.

STEERING

  • Hold LEFT or RIGHT, or hold the left or right half of the picture. Keys: arrow keys or A and D.
  • Steering takes you up the wall and, if you keep going, over the roof. Let go and you slide back to the floor.
  • Bends push you toward the outside wall. The faster you go, the harder the push.
  • STEER sets how quickly the sled moves round the wall: SLOW, MEDIUM or FAST. It is on the pause screen and the start screen.
  • PAUSE is at the top. Nothing sits under the three big keys while you race. TONE and SOUND come back when you pause.

SPEED

  • The sled always pulls toward its top speed.
  • A gold pad nearly doubles your speed for about two seconds. While it lasts you smash straight through blocks and purple sleds. Gates still stop you, and you have far less time to see what is coming.
  • The blue line on a bend adds speed for as long as you stay between its two thin rails.
  • A crash cuts your speed to a third. You flash for a moment and cannot crash again while flashing.

IN THE WAY

  • BLOCKS: pink boxes on the wall. Go round.
  • GATES: a pink wall across the whole tunnel with one gap. The gap's edges are gold.
  • PURPLE SLEDS: slower than you, drifting a little. Pass them.

OPEN AIR

  • Some stretches have no roof: a wide half-pipe under the sky, with a white rail along each rim.
  • Above the rim you lose half your speed until you slide back in. The bends out here push you toward it.

THE HUNTER DRONE

  • In the last two heats an orange drone joins you for part of the tunnel. It flies ahead down the middle and swings round to follow you.
  • Its dotted line shows where it is aiming. When the line turns white it drops there: usually a pair of blocks, and every third time a whole gate with the gap off to one side.
  • Keep changing sides and the blocks miss. It gives up after ten or more drops, or a shock ring destroys it.

THE SHOCK RING

  • You start a league with one shock ring and can hold three.
  • Every fifth star adds one.
  • SHOCK RING (the middle button, or Space) destroys blocks, gates and purple sleds for a short way ahead, and the drone if it is there. Pads and stars are untouched.

SCORE

  • Each heat scores the time you had left, ten points a second.
  • Your best league score is kept on this device. A league is not saved part-way: it takes about five minutes.
  • RESTART drops the league. It is on the pause screen and asks you to tap twice.
