LONGSHOT

HELP
  • Two guns on a ridge. Take turns firing until one is destroyed. First to three rounds wins the match.
  • Set BARREL (the angle) and POWDER (the force), then FIRE.
  • The wind pushes the shell. The arrow at the top and the drifting clouds show which way and how hard.
  • The bar at the top is your time. When it runs out the gun fires as it is set.
  • The closer the shell lands, the more damage. A direct hit destroys a gun outright.
  • Your last shot leaves a dotted trail, so you can correct from it.
ALL THE RULES

SETTING UP

  • ONE PLAYER: you are the cyan gun on the left, against the computer's red gun.
  • TWO PLAYERS: share the device. Cyan is player one, red is player two.
  • RANK sets the time you get for each shot: PRIVATE 60 seconds, CAPTAIN 30, GENERAL 15. In a one-player game it also sets how well the computer shoots.

YOUR SHOT

  • BARREL runs from 0 (flat) to 90 (straight up), always measured toward the enemy.
  • POWDER runs from 1 to 99.
  • Buttons change either by 1 or by 5. Hold a button to keep going. Keys: left and right for barrel, up and down for powder, Shift for steps of 5, Space to fire.
  • Both settings stay where you left them for your next shot.

WIND

  • The wind is shown as a number and an arrow. It pushes the shell sideways the whole time it is in the air, so high, slow shots drift most.
  • It shifts a little after every shot and is set fresh each round.

THE SHELL

  • A shell that goes off the top keeps flying and comes back down; a mark at the top edge follows it.
  • A shell that goes off either side is gone.
  • A shell that lands blows a crater out of the hill. Trees stop shells and are blown away too. A gun with the ground blown out from under it drops.

DAMAGE

  • Each gun has 100 hull. A direct hit takes all of it.
  • A near miss takes up to 75, falling off to nothing about a gun's length away.
  • You can damage your own gun.

THE MATCH

  • Each round has new hills, trees, gun positions and wind. The first shot alternates between rounds.
  • The first gun to win three rounds wins the match.
  • RESTART MATCH, under the controls, drops the match and goes back to the start screen. It asks you to tap twice.

SAVING

Nothing is saved. A match takes a few minutes.
