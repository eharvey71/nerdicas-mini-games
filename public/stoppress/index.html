STOP THE PRESS

HELP
  • Tap the picture to walk. Tap a thing to look at it, or a door to go through.
  • Pick LOOK, TAKE, USE or TALK, then tap a thing.
  • Tap something in your pockets, then tap what to use it on.
  • Stuck? HINT nudges you. LABELS shows what can be tapped.
ALL THE RULES

THE STORY

You are Vane, night reporter at THE WEEKLY PECULIAR. An hour ago you invented tomorrow's headline, MOON MAN LIVES IN OUR BASEMENT. The front page locks at midnight, and the editor wants a photograph.

GETTING ABOUT

  • Tap the floor to walk there.
  • Tap a door or stairway to go through it.
  • There are six places: the newsroom, the editor's office, the hallway, the darkroom, the basement and the roof.

THE FOUR VERBS

  • LOOK: describes a thing. With no verb chosen, a tap looks.
  • TAKE: picks a thing up, if it can be picked up.
  • USE: works a thing: a machine, a lever, a door.
  • TALK: speaks to someone. You may get a choice of things to say.

The line under the picture shows what you are about to do.

YOUR POCKETS

Tap a thing in your pockets to hold it; it turns yellow. Then tap something in the picture, or another thing in your pockets, to use the two together. Tap it again to put it away. LOOK then a pocket thing describes it.

HINT AND LABELS

HINT gives a gentle nudge toward the next step. Press it again without making progress and it says plainly what to do. LABELS draws a name on everything that can be tapped.

NO DEAD ENDS

You cannot die, lose, or throw away something you need. The clock moves only when you make progress.

SAVING

The game saves itself after every action, on this device. One or two things are hidden in a different place each new game.
