DUSTHAVEN

HELP
  • You run a mining outpost on a dead world. Pick a goal, then build month by month.
  • Tap a building button, then tap squares to place it. Everything must join the lander by tubes or by touching.
  • Colonists need air, water, food and beds. Buildings need power, and most need crew.
  • The SUPPLY row shows what you will have left over each month. Red means short, and people die.
  • Mines sit on ore (yellow) and earn credits. Ice wells sit on or beside ice (blue).
  • END MONTH runs the colony: income, arrivals, and whatever the planet throws at you.
  • UNDO takes back anything placed this month. A building marked ! is not working: tap it to see why.
  • The build buttons run down the left edge of the board. BUILD hides and shows them.
  • The numbers across the top of the board are the short version, and the newest message shows along the bottom. REPORT has the full status, the whole log and these notes.
  • Zoomed in, drag the map to move it. Everything laid over the map fades out of the way while you drag.
  • TILT has a TOP setting that fills a tall screen. Turn the phone sideways for a wide board with everything beside it.
ALL THE RULES

GOALS

  • CHARTER: reach 60 colonists by the end of month 36.
  • DEBT: you owe 4000 credits. Pay it all by the end of month 40, in parts if you like.
  • HOLD OUT: keep at least 20 colonists alive to the end of month 48. Storms come twice as often.
  • The run is lost if the deadline passes, or if nobody is left alive.

BUILDING

  • Build on open dust. Rock and craters cannot be built on.
  • A building only works if it is linked to the lander: through tubes, or by standing next to another linked building or tube. Diagonals do not link.
  • Costs are paid at once. Every building also costs upkeep each month.
  • UNDO refunds anything placed this month. REMOVE takes away a building or tube for good, with no refund.

THE BUILDINGS

  • LD Lander: where you start. Beds for 6, with enough power, air and water for those six and a little over. It cannot be removed.
  • TUBE, 10: links things.
  • HB Habitat, 120: beds for 8. Uses 1 power.
  • SO Solar array, 80: 4 power. Only a quarter of that in a dust storm.
  • RX Reactor, 420: 16 power in any weather. Needs 2 crew. Upkeep 18.
  • AP Air plant, 150: air for 10. Uses 2 power, 1 crew.
  • IW Ice well, 140: water for 20 colonists. Must stand on ice or right beside it. Uses 2 power, 1 crew.
  • FD Farm dome, 130: food for 7. Uses 1 power, water for 6, and 2 crew.
  • MN Mine, 220: must stand on ore. Earns 110 credits a month. Uses 3 power, 3 crew.
  • CL Clinic, 180: covers habitats within 4 squares. Raises morale and blunts outbreaks. Uses 1 power, 2 crew.

SUPPLY

  • Each colonist uses 1 air, 1 food and half a water every month.
  • POWER, AIR and WATER show what is left over after everyone and everything is supplied. FOOD shows the store and how it changes each month.
  • Short of power: buildings switch off, mines first, then clinics, farms, habitats, ice wells, and air plants last.
  • Short of crew: buildings stand idle, mines first, reactors last.
  • Short of air or water at month's end: colonists die. No food in the store: about a third of those unfed die.

PEOPLE AND MONEY

  • Each colonist pays 14 credits a month.
  • New colonists land each month if there are free beds, nothing is short, and morale is 40 or more. Bigger colonies draw more.
  • Colonists without a bed leave.
  • Morale starts at 60. Clinics covering your habitats raise it; every shortage and every death lowers it.

THE PLANET

  • DUST STORM: warned a month ahead, lasts one or two months. Solar arrays give a quarter of their power.
  • BREAKDOWN: a building stops. Tap it twice to repair it for 60 credits, or it mends itself after two months.
  • METEOR: destroys one building or tube and leaves a crater.
  • OUTBREAK: 15 in 100 colonists die, or 5 in 100 if every habitat is covered by a clinic.
  • SUPPLY DROP: 150 credits from orbit.
  • Trouble grows more likely as the months pass.

SAVING

The colony saves itself at the end of every month, on this device.
