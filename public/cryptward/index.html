CRYPTWARD

HELP
  • Pick one to four heroes. They play quest after quest, with a shop in between.
  • Each quest is a new crypt with a new task, told to you at the start and shown in the QUEST row.
  • Each hero rolls two dice to move and gets one action: attack, cast, or search.
  • Tap a lit square to walk there. Tap a monster beside you to attack it.
  • At a closed door it flashes yellow and an OPEN DOOR button appears. A red door is locked: find the key.
  • Stand beside furniture (the tan blocks) and SEARCH it for gold, potions, gear, clues and quest items.
  • Attackers count skulls; defenders cancel them with shields. Heroes use white shields, monsters black.
  • After the heroes comes the monsters' turn. A banner says what kind of turn it is.
  • Drag the board to look around. TURN VIEW spins it, TILT changes the angle, ZOOM shows the whole board.
ALL THE RULES

THE CAMPAIGN

  • The same party plays one quest after another. Each quest is a little harder than the last.
  • The last room's guard is sized to the party: a small or new party meets fewer and weaker guards there, and the rest of the crypt carries more of the danger instead. Gargoyles only guard the last room from the second quest on.
  • Winning pays 150 gold on top of what you found. Gold is shared by the party.
  • Between quests the shop sells healing potions (100), a better weapon (250, +1 attack die) and better armour (300, +1 defend die). A hero can hold at most two of each upgrade.
  • Heroes start every quest at full body with all their spells. A hero who died is replaced by a recruit with plain gear.
  • If every hero dies in a quest, the campaign is over.

QUESTS

  • KILL THE WARLOCK: it waits in the deepest room. The quest ends when it dies.
  • FREE THE CAPTIVE: reach the captive in the deepest room, then walk that hero back to the stairs.
  • FIND THE RELIC: it is in a chest in the deepest room. Search the chest, then carry it to the stairs.
  • BREAK THE DARK ALTAR: stand beside it and strike it twice (two actions), then get any hero to the stairs.
  • FIND THE SHARDS: three are hidden in furniture in three rooms. Find them all, then get any hero to the stairs.
  • FIND THE WAY OUT: a second stair, marked OUT, is in the deepest room. Each hero who steps on it leaves. The quest is won when no hero is left on the board and at least one got out.
  • Often the deepest room is locked. Its doors are red. The key is in a piece of furniture or carried by a monster somewhere else; killing that monster drops it.

HEROES

  • BARBARIAN: 3 attack dice, 2 defend, 8 body.
  • DWARF: 2 attack, 2 defend, 7 body. Spots hidden traps within two squares.
  • ELF: 2 attack, 2 defend, 6 body. Spells: MEND, SWIFT.
  • MAGE: 1 attack, 2 defend, 4 body. Spells: FIREBOLT twice, MEND, SLUMBER, STONE SKIN.

A HERO'S TURN

  • Movement is rolled for you: two dice, shown under the board with the moves you have left. Squares you can reach are lit. No diagonal steps.
  • You may pass through other heroes but not through monsters or furniture, and may not stop on an occupied square.
  • One action per turn. Act before you move or after, not in the middle: acting after you have moved ends your movement.
  • Opening a door and drinking a potion are free.
  • UNDO MOVE takes back the last walk, as long as it sprang no trap and brought no new monster into view. Dice, doors, searches and spells cannot be undone.
  • A lone hero gets two actions each turn.

DOORS

  • A door you can open flashes yellow with an arrow over it, and an OPEN DOOR button appears. Tapping your own hero, or the square behind the door, opens it too.
  • To open a door a hero must stand on the square in front of it. A hero cannot open a door past a friend who is standing there: the friend opens it on their own turn, and after that others may walk through them.
  • Red doors are locked and need the key. Once any hero has the key, every hero can open them.
  • Secret doors look like wall until a room search finds them. They only ever hide side rooms with extra treasure, never something a quest needs.

FURNITURE

  • Furniture blocks the square it stands on. To search a piece, stand beside it and press SEARCH, or tap it. Searching is an action, needs the room clear of monsters, and works once per piece. Searched pieces turn grey.
  • CH Chest: gold, sometimes a potion, sometimes a trap.
  • BK Bookcase: a clue if any are left, otherwise a spell scroll any hero can cast once. Clues mark rooms on the board in yellow or violet: first the quest room, then the room with the key, then the shard rooms.
  • WR Weapon rack: +1 attack die, up to two above what the hero started with.
  • TB Tomb: gold, a shield (+1 defend die), or a mummy that attacks at once.
  • AB Alchemist's bench: a healing potion.
  • TA Table and TH Throne: nothing. They are only in the way.
  • AL Dark altar: the target of one quest.
  • Rooms have a kind, named when the door opens: armory, library, crypt, barracks, shrine, storeroom, workroom. The kind decides the furniture and leans the monsters, so a crypt holds tombs and the undead.

SEARCHING A ROOM

  • With no furniture beside you, SEARCH looks over the room, or five squares of corridor.
  • It finds and disarms every trap there, and finds any secret door. Found traps are marked with a violet X and are safe to cross.
  • Stepping on a hidden trap costs 1 body and ends the move.

FIGHTING

  • You can only attack a monster directly beside you, not diagonally and not through a wall.
  • Each die has three skulls, two white shields and one black shield.
  • The attacker rolls its attack dice and counts skulls. The defender rolls its defend dice: heroes count white shields, monsters count black. Each shield cancels a skull; the rest come off body.
  • The dice are shown under the board and written in the log.
  • A hero about to die drinks a potion automatically if carrying one.

MONSTERS

  • Each piece carries two letters. Numbers are squares moved, attack dice, defend dice, body.
  • GO Goblin: 10, 2, 1, 1.
  • OR Orc: 8, 3, 2, 1.
  • SK Skeleton: 6, 2, 2, 1.
  • ZO Zombie: 5, 2, 3, 1.
  • BF Bog Fiend: 6, 3, 3, 2.
  • MU Mummy: 4, 3, 4, 2.
  • DK Dread Knight: 7, 4, 4, 3.
  • GA Gargoyle: 6, 4, 5, 3.
  • WL Warlock: 6, 4, 4, 5. Throws a 2-dice bolt at any hero it can see.
  • A white bar over a monster shows its remaining body when it has more than 1.

SPELLS

  • Casting is an action. Each spell or scroll is used once per quest.
  • FIREBOLT: 2 damage to a monster in sight. It rolls one die to block.
  • MEND: restores 4 body to any hero.
  • SWIFT: roll your movement again and add it.
  • SLUMBER: a monster in sight sleeps. It cannot act or defend, and each monsters' turn it has an even chance to wake. The Warlock is immune.
  • STONE SKIN: a hero gets 2 extra defend dice until next wounded.

THE MONSTERS' TURN

  • There is no player for the monsters. After the last hero has moved, the game takes one turn for all of them. A banner on the board names the kind of turn, and the log lists every move and roll.
  • Only monsters you have uncovered act. Those behind closed doors wait. Monsters never open doors.
  • NORMAL turn, the usual one: a monster beside a hero attacks the weakest hero beside it. A monster that can reach a hero this turn moves and attacks the nearest. The rest hold their room.
  • CUNNING turn: every monster that can reach the hero with the least body goes for that hero. Monsters that cannot reach anyone fall back to guard the quest room.
  • REINFORCEMENTS: a wandering monster appears at the open door farthest from the heroes, then a normal turn follows. Wanderers do not hold a room; they hunt you.
  • How often each comes up depends on the party. With four heroes about 1 turn in 4 is cunning and 1 in 25 brings reinforcements. With one hero about 1 in 20 is cunning and 1 in 40 brings reinforcements.
  • Fewer heroes also means fewer monsters, a potion each for parties of two or one, and two actions a turn for a lone hero.

SAVING

The quest saves itself at the end of every round, and the campaign saves after every quest and purchase, on this device.
