CRYPTWARD

HELP
  • Pick one to four heroes. Each quest is a new dungeon with a new goal.
  • Each hero rolls two dice to move, and gets one action: attack, cast, or search.
  • Tap a lit square to walk there. Tap a monster beside you to attack it. Tap the square behind an amber door to open it.
  • Drag the board to look around. TURN VIEW spins it a quarter turn, TILT changes the angle, ZOOM switches between close and the whole board.
  • UNDO MOVE takes back your last walk.
  • Attackers count skulls; defenders cancel them with shields. Heroes use white shields, monsters black.
  • After the heroes, the Deck turns a card and the monsters act.
  • Heroes are cyan, and the one whose turn it is turns gold with an arrow over it. Monsters are red, the Warlock magenta, the stairs pale blue.
ALL THE RULES

QUESTS

  • SLAY: kill the Warlock in the deepest room.
  • RESCUE: reach the captive in the deepest room, then bring them back to the stairs.
  • RELIC: search the deepest room to find the relic, then carry it back to the stairs.
  • The quest is lost when every hero is dead.

HEROES

  • REAVER: 3 attack dice, 2 defend, 8 body.
  • DELVER: 2 attack, 2 defend, 7 body. Spots hidden traps within two squares.
  • STRIDER: 2 attack, 2 defend, 6 body. Spells: MEND, SWIFT.
  • MAGUS: 1 attack, 2 defend, 4 body. Spells: FIREBOLT twice, MEND, SLUMBER, STONE SKIN.

A HERO'S TURN

  • Movement is rolled for you: two dice. Squares you can reach are lit. No diagonal steps.
  • You may pass through other heroes but not through monsters, and may not stop on an occupied square.
  • One action per turn. Act before you move or after, not in the middle: acting after you have moved ends your movement.
  • Opening a door and drinking a potion are free.
  • Your two movement dice and the moves you have left are shown under the board.
  • UNDO MOVE takes back the last walk, as long as it sprang no trap and brought no new monster into view. Dice, doors, searches and spells cannot be undone.
  • A lone hero gets two actions each turn.

FIGHTING

  • You can only attack a monster directly beside you, not diagonally and not through a wall.
  • Each die has three skulls, two white shields and one black shield.
  • The attacker rolls its attack dice and counts skulls. The defender rolls its defend dice: heroes count white shields, monsters count black. Each shield cancels a skull; the rest come off body.
  • The dice are shown under the board and written in the log.
  • A hero about to die drinks a potion automatically if carrying one.

SEARCHING

  • SEARCH is an action and needs the room or corridor around you to be free of monsters.
  • It finds and disarms every trap in the room, or within five squares in a corridor.
  • The first search of each room also turns up treasure: gold, a potion, a better weapon or shield, nothing, a trap, or a wandering monster that attacks at once.
  • Stepping on a hidden trap costs 1 body and ends the move.

SPELLS

  • Casting is an action. Each spell is used once per quest.
  • FIREBOLT: 2 damage to a monster in sight. It rolls one die to block.
  • MEND: restores 4 body to any hero.
  • SWIFT: roll your movement again and add it.
  • SLUMBER: a monster in sight sleeps. It cannot act or defend, and each Deck turn has an even chance to wake. The Warlock is immune.
  • STONE SKIN: a hero gets 2 extra defend dice until next wounded.

THE DECK

  • After the last hero, one card is turned from a deck of playing cards.
  • 2 to 10: a normal turn. A monster beside a hero attacks the weakest one. A monster that can reach a hero moves and attacks the nearest. Others hold their room. Monsters never open doors.
  • Face card: a cunning turn. Every monster that can goes for the hero with the least body. Those that cannot reach fall back to guard the quest room.
  • Ace: a wandering monster appears at the open door farthest from the heroes, then a normal turn follows. Wanderers hunt you.
  • The deck is built for the party: 36 number cards always, with 12 face cards and 2 aces for four heroes, 8 and 2 for three, 4 and 1 for two, 2 and 1 for one.
  • Fewer heroes also means fewer monsters, a potion each for parties of two or one, and two actions a turn for a lone hero.

SAVING

The quest saves itself at the end of every round, on this device.
