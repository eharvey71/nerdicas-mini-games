MARCHLANDS

Each turn has three steps: place new armies, attack, then make one move. Tap one of your countries, then a neighbour to attack it. A small block is 1 army; a large hollow block is 5. You get more armies for holding more countries and for keeping them joined up. The move step shifts armies from one of your countries to another that is joined to it by your own land; one army always stays behind, and you get one such move a turn. Take every country to win. The arrow in the list shows whose move it is.

How a battle is decided: each roll, the attacker throws up to 3 dice (one fewer than the armies in the attacking country) and the defender up to 2. The highest dice are compared, then the next highest. The higher die wins and the loser removes one army; a tie goes to the defender. The log shows every roll, for example YOU 6 4 2 v BLUE 5 4 | 6>5 BLUE -1 | 4=4 YOU -1. Scroll the log to read back.
