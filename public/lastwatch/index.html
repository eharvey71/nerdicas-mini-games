LASTWATCH

HELP
  • MISSION on the title screen picks the job: FETCH, SWEEP, BOMB, HOLD, RANDOM, or CAMPAIGN (five in a row with the same squad). The line under the map says what to do.
  • FETCH: carry the gold core from the wreck back to the landing zone at the bottom, then press EVAC.
  • Each soldier has two actions a turn. Blue squares cost one, yellow squares cost both.
  • Tap a hostile to aim. The number is the chance to hit. Firing ends that soldier's turn.
  • Stand beside walls and crates. Cover only works on the side the shot comes from.
  • OVERWATCH shoots the first hostile that moves in view. HUNKER makes cover count for more.
  • In FETCH, anyone outside the landing zone when you EVAC is left behind.
  • Drag the map to move it. VIEW, TURN VIEW, ZOOM and TILT sit at its bottom right. VIEW: FLAT shows the whole map from above.
ALL THE RULES

THE FOUR MISSIONS

  • FETCH: a craft is down and its core is still inside. Walk onto the core to lift it, carry it to the landing zone, and press EVAC. Lifting it brings drops. Anyone outside the zone at EVAC is left behind.
  • SWEEP: kill every hostile on the map, including the ones in the wreck. The count is in the status line. It ends the moment the last one falls.
  • BOMB: a device is ticking in the wreck. A soldier in a square touching it can DISARM, which ends that soldier's turn. Two DISARMs finish it. The first one sets off an alarm and brings drops. The turn limit is short.
  • HOLD: stay in the landing zone until the turns run out. The groups on the map come at you one after another, and drops land near the zone. If no soldier is in the zone at the end of a hostile turn, the mission fails.
  • RANDOM picks one of the four when you start.
  • Every mission has a turn limit, shown as TURN in the status line. In HOLD the limit is how long you must last.

CAMPAIGN

  • Five missions in a row: one of each kind in a random order, then one more.
  • Soldiers who come home keep their wounds, get 2 health back before the next mission, and gain a stripe after each win. A stripe is 3 aim, up to three stripes.
  • Soldiers who are killed or left behind are replaced by new ones at full health with no stripes.
  • A failed mission does not end the campaign. Whoever is still alive carries on, but nobody counts as home for that mission and nobody gains a stripe.
  • It gets harder as it goes: hostiles shoot a little better each mission. On ROOKIE, missions four and five also have one extra group.
  • The score is the total who came home, out of 20. The game is saved between missions.

TURNS AND ACTIONS

  • You move all your soldiers, then the hostiles move.
  • Each soldier has two actions. A move into a blue square uses one. A dash into a yellow square uses both.
  • FIRE, OVERWATCH, HUNKER and GRENADE need one action and end that soldier's turn. MEDKIT uses one action.
  • Tap a square once to see what it costs and who could shoot you there. Tap it again to go.
  • A move stops early if a new hostile comes into view.
  • UNDO MOVE takes back the last move, as long as nothing happened during it.

SHOOTING

  • The chance to hit starts at the soldier's aim. Range, cover and other things take away from it. The line under the map shows the sum when you aim.
  • Half cover (crates, drawn as a box with a slash) takes 20 off. Full cover (walls) takes 40 off.
  • Cover only counts if it is next to the target on the side the shot comes from. Get round the side and it counts for nothing.
  • A target with no cover takes 1 extra damage.
  • A soldier beside a wall can lean out to see and shoot round it.
  • Shots make noise, and noise wakes hostiles nearby.

THE SQUAD

  • RIFLE: steady at medium range. 6 health.
  • SCOUT: moves furthest. Shotgun: heavy damage close up, poor at range. 5 health.
  • SNIPER: no range penalty, but 20 off after moving and 20 off against anything very close. 4 health.
  • MEDIC: two medkits, each heals 4 on the medic or a soldier in a touching square. 6 health.
  • Everyone carries one grenade: 3 damage in a 3 by 3 box, to friend and foe, and it flattens crates.

OVERWATCH AND HUNKER

  • OVERWATCH: the soldier fires once at the first hostile that moves in view, at 15 off and ignoring its cover. Marked by a small ring.
  • Hostiles do the same. One with a ring will fire at the first soldier that moves in its view.
  • HUNKER: shots at this soldier through cover lose another 20, until your next turn. Marked by a bar.

THE HOSTILES

  • They stand in groups and do nothing until they see you, hear a shot, or are hit. When first seen they run for cover.
  • DRONE (diamond): fast and weak, tries to get round your cover.
  • TROOPER (triangle): shoots from cover.
  • BRUTE (square): no gun. Runs at the nearest soldier and claws for 3 to 4. Cover does not help.
  • WARDEN (hexagon): guards the core. Tough and accurate.
  • Once the core is lifted, drop flares land. Hostiles arrive at a flare after your next turn, already on overwatch.

RANKS

  • ROOKIE: the training rank. Fewer hostiles and no Brutes. They shoot much worse and do 1 less damage. A group you surprise loses its next turn. Shots carry less far. Every soldier has 2 extra health.
  • VETERAN: more hostiles, normal shots, fewer turns.
  • COMMANDER: the most hostiles, a guard with the Warden, the fewest turns.

THE END

The score is how many of the four come home. In FETCH that means soldiers inside the landing zone at EVAC. In the others it is everyone still alive. The mission fails if everyone is killed, or the turns run out in FETCH, SWEEP or BOMB. The best score is kept for each mission and rank.
