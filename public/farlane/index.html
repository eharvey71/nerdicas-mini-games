FARLANE

HELP
  • Pick a goal: clear a debt, kill the raider flagship, or win renown.
  • Buy goods where they are cheap, tap a star on the chart, jump, and sell where they are dear.
  • Raiders may meet you on arrival: fight them, run, or dump your cargo.
  • In the cockpit, stick left and right roll the ship, down pulls the nose up. FIRE shoots while held. THRUST is a switch: tap it on, tap it off. Raiders are red.
  • To dock, fly round to the front of the station, line up with the dotted frames, and go through the slot slowly.
  • Spend profit at the shipyard. Hull at zero ends the run.
ALL THE RULES

GOALS

  • DEBT: you owe the lender 8000 credits. Pay it all at any station by day 100. You can pay in parts.
  • FLAGSHIP: the raider flagship sits at the star marked in magenta. Jump there and destroy it. It has two escorts and hits hard, so build your ship first.
  • RENOWN: earn 450 renown. Kills give 10, 20 or 40 points by raider size, and every 50 credits of trading profit gives 1.

Raiders grow stronger and more numerous every 30 days, whatever the goal.

THE CHART

  • Ten stars, new every run. The ring is how far your fuel reaches. Tap a star to select it, then JUMP.
  • A jump burns fuel equal to its distance and takes that many days, rounded up.
  • Star colour and letter show what it is: F farm, M mine, G forge, L lab. The bars beside the name are danger, 0 to 3.

TRADING

  • Farms make food cheaply and pay well for machines and medicine.
  • Mines make ore and pay well for food and machines.
  • Forges make alloys and machines and pay well for ore and luxuries.
  • Labs make medicine and luxuries and pay well for alloys and food.
  • The market shows the price here beside the price at the star you selected: the last price you saw there, or LOW / HIGH if you have never been.
  • Each unit you sell pushes that price down a little, and each unit you buy pushes it up. Prices recover over a few days.
  • Stations pay 5% less than they charge.

RAIDERS

  • FIGHT takes you to the cockpit.
  • RUN works more often with a better engine. If it fails, the fight starts with your aft shield halved.
  • DUMP CARGO needs at least 3 units in the hold. You lose all of it and the raiders leave.
  • Each kill pays a bounty. Wrecks sometimes leave cargo.

THE COCKPIT

  • The stick, arrow keys or WASD fly the ship. Left and right roll it. Down pulls the nose up and up pushes it down. To turn, roll until the target is above or below the crosshair, then pull or push.
  • FIRE or Space shoots while held.
  • THRUST is a switch. Tap it once and the engine stays on; tap again and you slow to a crawl. On a keyboard, hold Shift instead, or press T to switch it.
  • The button under FIRE flips up and down on the stick.
  • TURN, under THRUST, sets how quickly the ship rolls and pitches: SLOW, MEDIUM or FAST. Nothing else changes speed.
  • The laser hits what is under the crosshair, out to range 450. It runs off a charge (LAS) that refills when you stop firing.
  • Brackets mark the nearest raider. An arrow at the screen edge points to it when it is out of view.
  • The scanner at the bottom shows what is around you from above: top of the oval is ahead, bottom is behind.
  • Hits from ahead drain the FORE shield, hits from behind drain AFT. With a shield gone, hits go to the hull. Thrusting makes you harder to hit.
  • Shields refill at every station. Hull does not: repair it at the shipyard.

DOCKING

  • You arrive well away from the station and usually off to one side. The arrow at the screen edge points to it, with the range.
  • The slot is on one face only. Dotted frames stretch out from it and mark the way in.
  • Fly to the far end of the frames, turn to face the slot, and follow them in.
  • THRUST goes much faster here than in a fight, and it takes about two seconds to slow down. Switch it off early.
  • You must pass through the slot at speed 32 or less, heading nearly straight in.
  • The readout says ON LINE when your present heading passes squarely through the slot, and marks your speed TOO FAST when it is over the limit.
  • Hitting the station costs 12 hull and puts you back in front of it. The message says which went wrong: line, angle or speed.

SAVING

The run saves itself each time you dock, on this device. Death ends the run.
