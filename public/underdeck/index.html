  • On the fifth floor, kill the Warden to win.
  • Left stick walks and sidesteps. Right stick turns; push it up to fire. FIRE also shoots.

ALL THE RULES

GOAL

The reactor deck has gone quiet. Work down five random floors. Floors one to four each hide a keycard and a locked lift door; take the card to the door to go down. Floor five has no lift: the Warden waits in the reactor room, and killing it wins the run. Your time is kept, and the fastest win is remembered.

MOVING

  • Left stick: up and down walk, left and right sidestep.
  • Right stick: left and right turn. Pushing it up fires.
  • Keys: W S walk, A D sidestep, left and right arrows turn, Space fires, 1 2 3 pick a gun, P pauses.
  • TURN sets how quickly you turn: SLOW, MEDIUM or FAST.

ROOMS AND DOORS

  • Amber doors slide up when you or an enemy comes near, and close behind you. Enemies behind a closed door do not know you are there.
  • ARMORY: the floor's new gun, if there is one, and ammo.
  • MEDBAY: a medkit, and a med station on the wall (green cross). Walk into the station to restore full health, once.
  • SECURITY: a terminal on the wall (cyan screen). Walk into it to put the whole floor on your map, keycard and lift included.
  • GUARD POST: extra guards. Nothing to gain there but kills.
  • REACTOR: floor five. The Warden.

SHOOTING

  • You do not need to be exact: the shot goes to the nearest enemy close to the crosshair that you can see. A white X on the crosshair means a hit.
  • PISTOL: never runs out. Slow and weak.
  • SCATTERGUN: floor one. Uses shells. Very strong up close, weak far away, and forgiving to aim.
  • PULSE RIFLE: floor three. Uses cells. Fires fast.

ENEMIES

  • Each has its name and health bar over its head.
  • DRONE: a spinning diamond. Weak and quick. Rams you.
  • GUARD: a walking figure with a gun. It raises the gun and flashes yellow for most of a second before each shot.
  • TURRET: a tripod. Does not move. Shoots often.
  • BRUTE: a wide, heavy figure. Slow and tough. Hits very hard up close, so back away while you shoot.
  • WARDEN: floor five only. Tall, crowned, magenta. Shoots hard and hits harder up close.
  • Once an enemy has seen you it follows you through the maze.
  • Shots miss more often at long range. Breaking the line of sight before the flash ends makes the shot miss.

PICKUPS

  • Green cross: 25 health, up to 100. It stays put if you are at full health.
  • Yellow boxes: shells or cells. Cyan card: the keycard. White: a gun.
  • Health does not come back on its own. Zero ends the run.

SAVING

There is no saving. A run takes about ten minutes. PAUSE stops the clock.
