REGOLITH

  • Land on a flat pad, upright, with FALL 15 or less and DRIFT 10 or less.
  • Hold THRUST to slow your fall. ROTATE, then thrust, to stop sideways drift.
  • A highlighted number in the readout would crash you.
  • Beeping means fuel is under 20. No fuel, no engine.
ALL THE RULES

GOAL

Score as many points as you can before the fuel runs out. Every safe landing scores, then you launch again over a new map.

CONTROLS

  • THRUST: hold to fire the engine. It pushes the way the lander's top is pointing.
  • ROTATE: each tap turns the lander 15 degrees. Hold to keep turning.
  • Keys: arrows or A and D to rotate; up, W or space to thrust.

A SAFE LANDING

  • Both feet on a flat pad.
  • FALL 15 or less.
  • DRIFT 10 or less, in either direction.
  • TILT 15 or less: upright, or one click off.

Anything else is a crash, and the crash box names the number that was over its limit.

THE READOUT

  • SCORE: points this run.
  • FUEL: what is left of 100.
  • ALT: height above the ground under you.
  • DRIFT: sideways speed. Minus is leftward.
  • FALL: downward speed. Minus means you are climbing.
  • TILT: degrees off upright.

A number is highlighted while it is over its landing limit, and FUEL is highlighted when it is low.

THE BEEPING

One beep a second means fuel is under 20. When the tank is empty there is a last beep, the engine stops, and you fall.

PADS AND SCORE

Each map has three pads marked x2, x3 and x5. Narrower pads have the bigger number. A landing scores 50 times the pad's number, plus up to 50 more for a gentle touchdown. It also gives fuel back: 10, 15 or 25.

FUEL

A full tank is about 16 seconds of thrust. Fuel carries over from one landing to the next. A crash burns 20. The run ends when you crash with nothing left.

IT GETS HARDER

After each landing the ground is rougher, gravity is a little stronger, and you launch with more sideways drift to cancel.

THE VIEW

The picture zooms in when you are close to the ground. Fly off one side and you come back on the other.
